A 5% discount feels small.
The volume it demands is not.

Extra sales volume needed just to earn the same gross profit after a price cut — before the discount has created a single euro of upside.

Volume uplift required to break even — at a 30% gross margin
…and it gets steeper the thinner your margin
A discount is a bet on volume. Before approving one, ask a single question: where, exactly, is the extra volume coming from? If nobody can answer with a number, the discount is not a growth lever — it is a margin donation.
Method · Required volume uplift = d ÷ (m − d), where m = gross margin on price and d = discount, both as % of list price. Break-even = same total gross profit as before the cut; assumes variable-cost margin, no fixed-cost absorption or elasticity effects — real break-evens are usually worse once payment terms and rebates stack on top.
