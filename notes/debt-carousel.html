Five forces move a debt ratio

Budgets didn’t cut the debt. Inflation did.

All nine ran primary deficits over 2021–25. Inflation took 11 to 25 points of GDP off each ratio. That is more than real growth did in 6 of the nine.

Net interest as a share of government revenue

The US now spends 11.8% of its revenue on interest

At that speed, with yields where they are, the rate on the debt passes nominal growth by 2031 in the US, Britain, France and Italy. The IMF’s path has 1: Italy.

What to do with it

Three things to say, and one not to

1
“Flirting” is the right word. The average rate is still at or below growth; the market rate is above it; the average closes about 13% of that gap a year.
2
Rank by the budget gap, not the debt ratio. On debt, Japan and Italy lead. On the gap, the US leads by a distance.
3
Check the interest line in any 2031 debt path. At today’s yields the IMF’s rate for the US is 0.8 points low.
×
Not “they paid down pandemic debt”. Inflation did. It worked because it was a surprise, and a surprise cannot be planned for twice.
