📋 Revision Notes what changed after the review · Aug 2026

This build implements the review notes on the Toll Road example (e-mail, 4 Aug 2026). Each point below says what changed and links to where it lives on the page.
Investment period
  • Debt sized on the S&U bottom line — the loan is now gearing × total uses (including IDC, financing fees and the DSRA pre-funding), solved iteratively because IDC and facility size depend on each other. At 70% gearing, debt is exactly 70% of total uses. → Sources & Uses
  • IDC on the outstanding balance — interest during construction now accrues monthly on the drawn balance of each tranche (₺ and USD separately, each at its own rate), following the actual drawdown path rather than a yearly approximation. Monthly is this model's day-count equivalent; a literal per-day accrual would change the figures only marginally. → Drawdown
  • Capex drawdown schedule — a new input: linear, S-curve (default for the toll road), front- or back-loaded. It drives the draws, and with them IDC and commitment fees. → Inputs · Project
  • DSRA inside the funding plan — the 6-month pre-funding is now a Uses line funded at COD, so the first debt-service reserve is part of the construction budget; afterwards the reserve tracks next period's debt service. → Sources & Uses
  • “Opex during construction” removed from S&U — pre-COD overheads (utilities, staff and similar G&A) now sit inside Non-EPC, as suggested; the separate input is gone.
  • Zero cash flow during construction — with every use funded by a source, the project's net cash flow in the investment period is now zero by construction (greenfield logic); the cash flow statement shows it line by line. → Financial Statements
Operating period
  • Operating revenue model — toll-road revenue is now built bottom-up: vehicle categories × daily transits × tariff, with traffic growth, CPI-linked or fixed tariff escalation and an authority revenue share, all as inputs. The simple annual-revenue mode remains for other asset types. → Inputs · Revenue
  • DSCR flat at target in every year — the debt engine now runs at the debt-service payment frequency (semi-annual by default; annual / quarterly selectable) instead of annually. With sculpted repayment, coverage now sits on the target in all years — including year 1, which previously showed 1.14×. → DSCR Profile
  • CFADS = EBITDA − tax — redefined per the review; maintenance capex and DSRA movements moved below CFADS, into the equity cash flow. → DSCR Profile
General framework
  • USD base currency — the default display is now USD (₺ stays one click away): long-horizon ₺ figures are distorted by inflation.
  • WACC & Cost of Equity from components — the former “Project discount / Equity hurdle” inputs are replaced by risk-free rate, equity risk premium, beta and country risk premium: CoE = rf + β×ERP + CRP; WACC = (1−g)·CoE + g·kd·(1−tax). Project NPV discounts at WACC, Equity NPV at CoE, both on USD-equivalent flows. → Inputs · WACC
  • Financial statements — a new card with the income statement, cash flow and balance sheet in horizontal periodic layout (in that order), including FX revaluation of the USD debt and a balance-check row. → Financial Statements

DSCR Profile debt service coverage ratio · operating years

DSCR = CFADS ÷ DS. CFADS = Cash Flow Available for Debt Service = EBITDA − tax (maintenance capex and DSRA movements sit below CFADS, in the equity cash flow); DS = Debt Service (interest + scheduled principal), paid at the selected payment frequency — with DSCR-targeted sculpting the coverage is flat at the target every year, including year 1. A DSCR of 1.30× means operating cash flow covers that year's debt service 1.3 times over — the cushion lenders size the loan against.

Financial Statements income statement · cash flow · balance sheet

ProjeFin is an illustrative project finance (PF) model for infrastructure projects. Senior debt is sized as gearing × total uses (the Sources & Uses bottom line, including IDC, fees and the DSRA pre-funding); interest during construction (IDC) accrues monthly on the outstanding drawn balance of each tranche following the selected capex schedule. In the operating period revenue comes from a simple annual figure or a traffic × tariff build-up (vehicle categories, traffic growth, CPI- or fixed-escalated tariffs, authority revenue share); debt service runs at the selected payment frequency (semi-annual by default), repaid by annuity or sculpted to a target DSCR. CFADS = EBITDA − tax; maintenance capex and DSRA movements sit below CFADS in the equity cash flow. Debt is split into a ₺ tranche and a USD tranche: the USD leg accrues at its own rate and converts to ₺ at each year's USD/TRY, so a falling lira raises ₺ debt service — FX cover and the open-FX position show the mismatch. Outputs: unlevered Project IRR, levered Equity IRR, NPV at a component-built WACC / cost of equity (USD nominal, on USD-equivalent flows), DSCR (min/average), LLCR, and full periodic financial statements (income statement, cash flow, balance sheet). The base display currency is USD — long-horizon ₺ figures are distorted by inflation. All calculations run in the browser — this is not an official financial model or investment advice.
