The gap is not biology — it is practice. Norwegian and Swedish animals are not healthier by luck: vaccination, herd health planning, husbandry and prescribing discipline replaced routine antibiotics. Every country at the top of this chart has a working example of the alternative sitting inside the same single market.
Sources & method · European Medicines Agency, ESVAC — European Surveillance of Veterinary Antimicrobial Consumption, 13th and final report (published Nov 2023, 2022 data; the programme now continues under the EU Antimicrobial Sales & Use platform). Unit: mg of active substance per kg of estimated live biomass (PCU, Population Correction Unit) — a denominator that adjusts for each country's animal population and species mix, so it compares intensity of use, not market size. Sales data, not administered dose: it does not capture how many animals were treated, and species mix (poultry/pig-heavy countries sit structurally higher).
