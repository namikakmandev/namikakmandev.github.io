Parity: Cattle — the three-year wave

Measured against corn, a kilo of American beef is worth 6.2 times the feed it was worth at the December 1974 low. Behind that drift sits a cycle that has run four times in 55 years: feed gets expensive, herds are slaughtered, and three years later there isn't enough beef. This traces the meat-to-feed parity for the US, the EU and Türkiye on a common 2016 base, times the lag between feed cost and cattle price, and prices the cycle through a feedlot P&L. Every figure comes from the published series and is cross-checked against outside estimates — including the one place the model is thinner than it looks.

Interactive tool Veterinary medicines · Dispensing law · 34 countries · Sourced research

Who is allowed to sell the animal's medicine? A dispensing-rights map of Europe

A legal map, not a metric: in each of 34 European countries, may the vet practice sell the medicines it prescribes — or must a licensed pharmacy do it? The answer splits Europe into channels: vet practices dispense across most of the west and centre, licensed veterinary pharmacies carry the east and the Balkans, the Nordics decouple prescribing from selling (Finland allows hand-over only at the vet's own purchase price, profit prohibited), and Spain prohibits vet dispensing outright. Every country cites a statute or regulator; where a rule couldn't be pinned to its legal text, the map says "to verify" instead of guessing.

Big Mac parity map — the fast price signal, current to the latest half-year

The timeliness complement to the PPP explorer: Eurostat's full-basket price levels run about eighteen months behind, while The Economist's Big Mac index is published every January and July. This map shows how much the same burger costs versus the euro area at market exchange rates, for every edition back to 2000 — with euro countries painted as one bloc from the year each adopted the currency. The GLP-1 molecule landscape — what each one targets, and how far it has got

Eight molecules mapped by receptor target, development stage and indication. Two are approved for obesity; six are still in trials. The field began by activating the GLP-1 receptor alone and now targets four — pairing it with GIP, amylin or glucagon to add effects rather than raise the dose. Case study Economics · Pharma · Eurostat · OECD · Trade · Python

Pharma is 1% of every big economy. In Ireland it is 17%.

35 years of data for 41 countries. Pharmaceutical manufacturing is about 1% of every large economy and has been for three decades — the US 0.87%, Japan 0.66%, Germany 0.67%. Five small economies break the pattern, Ireland at 16.7%. Size and share are different questions: Germany's pharma industry adds €26bn to its economy, Denmark's adds €36bn from an economy a tenth the size. And of every €100 of income generated inside Ireland, €75 stays there. A high pharma share turns out to be exposure, not strength.

Statistics · Free tool · HTML · JS · Python

Correlation & Regression Check

Two unrelated random walks correlate at r = 0.96. Paste two or three series — CSV from Excel or Sheets — and see whether the relationship survives the checks that catch that: levels vs changes, sample size discounted for autocorrelation, methodology-break detection, and a Bonferroni-corrected lag scan. Then it fits the regression: the slope with Newey–West errors, residual diagnostics, any number of confounder controls with VIF checks, an Engle–Granger cointegration test, block-bootstrap intervals, out-of-sample validation, a session ledger that raises the significance bar for every check you run, .xlsx upload, and a one-click verification report. Runs entirely in the browser; the same engine re-validated every correlation in the cattle margin study.

The Pricing Game

Can you run pricing for a year? You are the pricing manager of a coffee brand in a four-brand market — a mainstream rival that fights back, a premium player, and a discounter that storms in mid-year. Every month brings an event (cost spikes, price cuts, booms, a shortage) and two decisions: your price and one strategic move, each with real effects on demand, trust and cash. Share is split by a logit choice model with a lag, customers punish gouging, and a coach grades every decision — then your P&L is scored against the autopilot. Can you beat it?
