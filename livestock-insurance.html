LIVESTOCK INSURANCE · STUDY IN PROGRESS

Who insures the herd?

Penetration, physical coverage and loss ratios of livestock insurance schemes, from the schemes' own published data. Türkiye first — TARSİM's annual reports are the best public dataset in this field; the US (USDA RMA) and Spain (ENESA) extractions are in progress.

Türkiye: from 3% of the herd to 41% in eleven years

Share of the national cattle herd covered by TARSİM cattle-life insurance.

Insured cattle head (TARSİM annual reports, key-figures tables) ÷ national cattle stock (FAOSTAT). 2013: 3% → 2024: 41%. 2025 insured head is published (10.0M, +45% on 2024) but the 2025 herd figure is not yet — the penetration point is therefore not drawn.

What the pool pays out

Paid losses as a share of written premium, as published — unitless, so twelve years of lira inflation cancel out.

Whole pool from 2017 (earlier reports do not publish paid loss); cattle line from 2021 (line-level tables begin there). 40% in 2024 → 77% in 2025 — the source reports the jump; this draft does not attribute a cause. Paid loss excludes outstanding claims; premiums are written, not earned.

Physical coverage

Insured animals in million head — quantities, immune to currency and inflation. Sheep & goats shown without a penetration line: the herd denominator is not yet in the data set.

One market ≠ one product

DATA EXTRACTED

Türkiye — TARSİM

State-subsidized pool. Mortality & disease cover for registered animals; ~50% premium subsidy. Annual reports publish head counts, premiums, claims.

IN PROGRESS

United States — USDA RMA

Subsidized price and margin programs (LRP, DRP, LGM), not mortality cover. Data served via the Summary of Business application; extraction route being built.

IN PROGRESS

Spain — Agroseguro / ENESA

Subsidized pool incl. livestock life lines. Insurer site blocks automated access; ministry (ENESA) contracting reports identified as the route.

NOT INSURANCE

Germany — Tierseuchenkassen

Compulsory public epidemic funds levied per animal. Low private-insurance uptake does not mean unmanaged risk — this category is kept separate by design.

Because the products differ (mortality vs price risk), loss ratios and penetration are compared within a scheme over time, not ranked across schemes.

Sources & method. TARSİM annual reports 2016, 2020, 2024, 2025 (tarsim.gov.tr, English PDFs) — key-figures tables give four years each; overlapping windows agreed with zero conflicts. Every extracted value carries its report and page in data/livestock-ins-tarsim.json. Herd denominator: FAOSTAT cattle stocks via Our World in Data (data/herd-cattle.json). Loss ratio = total paid loss ÷ total written premium, both as published; paid loss excludes outstanding claims. Data cut 11 Aug 2026. Personal analysis of public statistics; draft, not for distribution.
