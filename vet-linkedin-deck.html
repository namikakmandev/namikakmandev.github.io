METHOD

What exactly is being measured.

Producer price index
The average selling price manufacturers receive at the factory gate. Not pharmacy prices, not reimbursement, not net of rebates.
"Ingredient" =
Bulk active-ingredient manufacturing. US: NAICS 325411, medicinal & botanical — BLS PCU325411325411, monthly since 1982. EU: NACE C21.1.
"Medicine" =
Finished-dose preparation manufacturing. US: NAICS 325412 — BLS PCU325412325412, monthly since 1981. EU: NACE C21.2. TR: TÜİK Yİ-ÜFE NACE 21.2.
"Real" =
Deflated by US CPI-U or euro-area HICP, named per page. The two US indexes sit on different base months — so only changes between two named dates are ever quoted, never levels.

SCOPE & LIMITS

What this data can and cannot say.

Not veterinary-specific
Every series mixes human and animal products — BLS lines read "for Human Use"; Eurostat and TÜİK publish no split. A vet-only price index does not exist, anywhere.
Prices, not volumes
List-basis factory-gate prices. Nothing here says how much was sold, or at what net realisation after discounts.
EU: 14 annual points
Eurostat's preparations index starts 2010 and stops at 2023 — EU findings are directions, not timings, and say nothing about 2024–2026.
No levels, only changes
The two US indexes are based Jun 1981 and Jun 1982. Their raw ratio (4.89) becomes 1.08–2.57 depending on rebasing date — so no level or multiple is ever quoted.
