03 · What exactly is being measured

Factory-gate prices, from the statistical agencies.

Producer price index
The average selling price manufacturers receive at the factory gate. Not pharmacy prices, not reimbursement, not net of rebates or discounts.
"Ingredient" =
Bulk active pharmaceutical ingredient manufacturing — US industry NAICS 325411, medicinal & botanical. BLS PCU325411325411, monthly since 1982. EU: NACE C21.1.
"Medicine" =
Finished-dose pharmaceutical preparation manufacturing — US industry NAICS 325412. BLS PCU325412325412, monthly since 1981. EU: NACE C21.2. TR: TÜİK Yİ-ÜFE NACE 21.2.
"Real" =
Deflated by US CPI-U or euro-area HICP (named per page). The two US indexes sit on different base months — so their ratio has no meaningful level, and every figure here is a change between two named dates.

05 · United States · Whole pharma industry · 1982–2026

Two-thirds of the movement is the ingredient — not the price.

How this is measured: take the month-on-month change of the finished÷ingredient ratio and split its variance by source, net of co-movement. 65.8% of the ratio's motion traces to the ingredient side, across 527 monthly changes. A manufacturer watching its own selling price is watching the quieter half of its own margin.

07 · United States · Antibiotics · Dec 2009 – Jun 2026 · Real

The antibiotic line: two true readings, opposite directions.

Broad & medium spectrum antibiotics have their own BLS product-line index (WPU06380802, begins Dec 2009 — human and animal use together). On its own window:
Against its ingredient
+58% spread
Antibiotic price +12.0% real while the ingredient input fell −29.3% — the spread widened.
Against its peers
below the book
The whole finished book gained +29.4% and its own parent class +26.5% on the identical window — antibiotics +12.0%.
Reading: margin widened because the input got cheap, not because the line held price. Input deflation is a tailwind — it stops the day ingredient prices turn.

10 · Türkiye · Per 1% of lira depreciation

The margin takes the hit in the first two months.

Margin ratio = domestic preparations price ÷ imported input price. Per 1% depreciation it loses 0.64% immediately and 0.87% cumulatively by the next month — then the effect fades below measurability. Front-loaded, not repaid — which sets the hedging horizon at a quarter, not a year.

11 · Scope & limits — read before quoting

What this data can and cannot say.

Not veterinary-specific
Every series mixes human and animal products. BLS product lines read "for Human Use"; Eurostat and TÜİK publish no split. A veterinary-only price index does not exist, anywhere.
Prices, not volumes
List-basis factory-gate prices. Nothing here says how much was sold, or at what net realisation after discounts.
EU: 14 annual points
Eurostat's preparations index starts 2010 and stops at 2023. EU findings are directions, not timings — and say nothing about 2024–2026.
No levels, only changes
The two US indexes are based Jun 1981 and Jun 1982. Their raw ratio (4.89) moves between 1.08 and 2.57 depending on the rebasing date — so no level, multiple or margin is ever quoted here.
