10 · So what — one instruction per market

The margin came from the ingredient. That's a tailwind, not a strategy.

United States
Price off the ingredient cycle.
Two-thirds of the spread's movement is the input side — build it into the price calendar, not a finished-goods benchmark that moves later and less.
European Union
Defend volume. There is no spread to find.
Ingredient and finished prices fell within 1.2 points of each other over fourteen years.
Türkiye
Hedge the quarter. Reprice with the currency.
68% of a lira move is in your costs the same month; 17% reaches your price. Close the cadence gap.
