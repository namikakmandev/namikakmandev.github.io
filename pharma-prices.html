Cost per itch-free day — like-for-like blocks
Maintenance therapy for a ~20 kg dog. Each block holds four things constant — dose (the weight-matched strength: Apoquel 16 mg, Zenrelia 15 mg, Numelvi 21.6 mg, Cytopoint 40 mg), form, pack size, and channel (retail; clinic-administered pricing is excluded). Blocks are never compared with each other: a per-tablet price and a 100-pack unit price are different goods, and the bulk discount inside a single venue runs 4–31%. No pack size exists in all fifteen markets — the US, UK and Australia sell these tablets singly while continental Europe sells 20s and 100s — which is why the table is tiered rather than ranked. Cytopoint is a range because its label says every 4 to 8 weeks, not monthly; Apoquel additionally needs twice-daily dosing for 14 days (Zenrelia and Numelvi do not), adding ~47% to its first month, not included below.
