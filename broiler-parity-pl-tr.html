Method & honesty notes. Türkiye: TÜİK domestic PPI, processed & preserved poultry meat (C10.12) ÷ prepared farm-animal feeds (C10.91), scaled to TEPGE's official 2023 parity (3.48 kg feed per kg chicken) — the series behind the TR monitor. Poland: the same NACE pair from Eurostat sts_inppd_m (domestic output prices, 2021 = 100), fetched and rebuilt monthly by GitHub Actions (probe report: data/_pl-parity-probe.json). Both series are factory-gate, not farm-gate — one step removed from the grower; for integrated producers the two move together, but levels are indicative. The indexed chart compares dynamics only and is valid regardless of anchoring; the kg/kg chart shows a country only when its absolute level is anchored to an official parity. Base-year changes (Eurostat rebases, TÜİK 2003=100) affect levels, not the ratio's movement.
