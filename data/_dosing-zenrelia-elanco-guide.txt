Extreme itching (Severe/continuous episodes) 
My dog does not stop itching irrespective of surroundings or  
commands, and needs to be physically restrained from itching.
Severe itching (Prolonged episodes) 
Itching disrupts my dog’s sleep, eating, play, and exercise.  
Itching continues even when my dog is distracted.
Moderate itching (Frequent episodes) 
My dog is itchy periodically through the day, but not 
when eating, playing, exercising, or being distracted.  
Itching occurs at night when observed.
Mild itching (Semi-frequent episodes) 
My dog does not itch when eating, playing, exercising,  
or being distracted.
Very mild itching (Occasional episodes) 
My dog is only slightly itchier than what I consider normal.

Itching is not a  
problem for my dog.
