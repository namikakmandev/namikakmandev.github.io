The growth is in Central & Eastern Europe. Inflation ate the entire nominal increase in the EU’s big Western markets — in CEE, spending outran it by 40–65 points. For animal-health strategy: the West is a price & mix game; the volume story has moved East.
Sources & method · Eurostat, Economic Accounts for Agriculture (aact_eaa01), item AM205000 “Veterinary expenses”, € million at current prices — covers all livestock on farms, not cattle only; excludes companion-animal care. Real change = country series deflated by euro-area HICP annual mean (FRED CP0000EZ19M086NEST; +28.7% cumulative 2015→2025). Values in current EUR: FX effects included for non-euro countries. UK excluded (series ends 2020). 2025 = latest Eurostat EAA vintage. Reproducible from data/eu-vet-countries.json, data/eu-vet-expenses.json, data/eu-hicp.json (fetched via scripts/fetch.py); data cut-off Aug 2026.
