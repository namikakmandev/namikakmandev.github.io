The GLP-1 molecule landscape. What each one targets, and how far it has got.

GLP-1 medicines copy the gut hormones that tell the body it has eaten. They changed the treatment of type 2 diabetes, then of obesity. This is the field mapped by molecule rather than by company: which receptors each one targets, what stage it has reached, and which disease it is aimed at. No trade names, and no share prices — the commercial side of the same story is a separate study.

Method. Receptor targets, approval status and development stage are taken from company filings (10-Q, 8-K), company releases, regulatory announcements and industry landscape reviews published between December 2025 and August 2026. A molecule is named only where a filing or company release confirms it. Every figure on this page and in the carousel is read from data/glp1-molecules.json, which is written by scripts/glp1_molecules_carousel.py — so the page and the deck cannot drift apart, and the counts cannot contradict the table behind them.

Limits. Trial results are point-in-time and development stages change; several readouts are due. Weight-loss figures come from different trials with different populations, doses and durations, and are not head-to-head — the chart above shows them separately for that reason. This page describes the state of a research field. It is not medical advice, and nothing here is a recommendation about any treatment.
