Broiler Margin Trends

Each market against its own history: chicken-meat producer price over feed-grain producer price, annual, farm-gate, in local currency. The dashed line is that country's own 2015–2024 norm — above it margins are fat, below it producers are squeezed. Panels are not cross-comparable by design: feed mixes and price bases differ.

Method & honesty notes. Source: FAOSTAT Producer Prices (annual, LCU/tonne), fetched by scripts/fetch_broiler_annual.py. Parity = tonnes of the named feed grain one tonne of chicken buys. The grain is a proxy for compound feed — soymeal and premix sit outside it. Türkiye's meat series is on a "biological" (live-weight) basis, so its level is not a carcass ratio — read the trend. Egypt and Saudi Arabia were evaluated and left out: recent-year gaps, a ~2-year lag and heavily imported feed make their annual series a weak read (the data stays in data/broiler-annual.json). FAOSTAT lags ~2 years; for a live Türkiye read use the monthly Broiler Margin Monitor.
