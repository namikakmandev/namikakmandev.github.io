Vets in the household budget — and as an industry

Two separate lenses, two separate datasets. Basket weight: out of every 1,000 units households spend, how much goes to veterinary & pet services — a spending share, not a price. Industry: the veterinary sector itself — practices, turnover, employment per country. Neither measures a margin or markup anywhere; that is a separate assessment.

Sources: Eurostat. Basket weights: prc_hicp_inw — HICP item weights, categories CP0934 (pets and related products) and CP0935 (veterinary and other services for pets), annual, per-mille of each country's total consumption basket, 1996–2025; Türkiye transmits no weight for these categories, and a transmitted zero (Ireland in some years, Kosovo throughout) is treated as "not separately reported", never as a real zero. Industry: sbs_na_1a_se_r2 — structural business statistics, veterinary activities (NACE M75); legacy series, most countries end at 2020 (UK earlier) — each row shows its own reference year; later years live in Eurostat's successor business statistics and are not yet wired in. Turnover per person employed is Eurostat's published indicator; turnover per enterprise and staff per enterprise are simple ratios derived on this page and labelled as such. Nothing here measures veterinary prices (see the vet-CPI page for price trends) or distribution margins.
